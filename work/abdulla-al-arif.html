One visual identity across a group of construction, real estate and service companies.

I developed a unified visual identity for the AAAI Group’s diverse sectors, designing exhibition environments and high-profile print assets for milestones like the Annual Gathering Iftar, and modernising their visual language across construction and real estate.

I created a scalable design system that kept the brand consistent while highlighting the strengths of each subsidiary, from internal email campaigns and Instagram stories to magazines, posters and event banners.
