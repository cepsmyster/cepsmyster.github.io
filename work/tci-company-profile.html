Company profile and identity for an architecture group

Client
Top Concept International
Work
Brand identity, editorial
Sector
Architecture and engineering
Year
2026

108 pages for Top Concept International, and the identity system that holds them together.

Architecture, interiors and landscape each open on a full-bleed divider. Every project spread after it follows one grid of large and small renders, so a hundred pages read as one book.

Underneath sits the identity: the TCi wordmark and folded-square mark, a palette of greys, Montserrat and Inter. It carries through to business cards, wall signage, folders, pens and mugs.
