Design and technical presentation for a private villa estate

Client
Top Concept International
Work
Presentation design
Sector
Residential
Year
2026

From the site to the cost in one deck: a design and technical presentation for a waterfront villa estate, made at Top Concept International.

Project overview, schematic drawings, 3D renders of the main mansion and outdoor majlis, interior moodboards, then the timeline, cash flow and bill of quantities. The client never has to open a second file.

The renders run full width under a thin header, so the stone and the formal gardens read at full size. For the interiors, the moodboards switch to a darker, editorial layout.
