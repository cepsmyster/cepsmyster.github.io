A design and technical scope presentation for a waterfront villa estate, made at Top Concept International.

One deck takes the client from the site to the cost: project overview, schematic drawings, 3D renders of the main mansion and outdoor majlis, interior material moodboards, then the project timeline, cash flow and bill of quantities.

The renders run full width under a thin header so the stone architecture and formal gardens read at full size, and the moodboards switch to a darker, editorial layout for the interiors.
