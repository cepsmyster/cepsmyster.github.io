Concept design presentation for a forest resort

Client
Top Concept International
Work
Presentation design
Sector
Hospitality
Year
2026

74 slides that walk a client through a forest resort in India, made at Top Concept International.

It starts with the master plan: suites strung along a winding lagoon. Then one building at a time: amenities, restaurant and banquet, clubhouse, health club and suites. Each gets a colour-coded floor plan first, then the 3D concept views.

Material boards of tent fabric, natural wood, mud plaster and sandstone tie the renders together. A quiet stone-grey layout with small headers lets the warm interiors carry every slide.
