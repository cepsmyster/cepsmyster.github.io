Concept design presentation for a forest resort

Client
Top Concept International
Work
Presentation design
Sector
Hospitality
Year
2026

A 74-slide concept design presentation for a resort in India, made at Top Concept International.

The deck starts from the master plan, with suites strung along a winding lagoon, then walks through each building in turn: amenities, restaurant and banquet, clubhouse, health club and suites. Every building gets a colour-coded floor plan first and the 3D concept views after it.

Material boards of tent fabric, natural wood, mud plaster and sandstone tie the renders together, and a quiet stone-grey layout with small headers lets the warm interiors carry each slide.
