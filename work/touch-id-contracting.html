Brand identity for a contracting company

Touch ID Contracting
Work
Brand identity
Sector
Construction
Year

Two identity systems for Touch ID Contracting in Dubai: the touch id wordmark and the tid monogram.

The touch id wordmark hides a fingerprint in the o and is set in cinnamon orange with a striped barrier-tape pattern. It runs across business cards, building signage, company cars, buses, mugs and safety vests.

TID condenses the name into a boxed monogram in charcoal and bronze, built for the places a contractor’s name is seen most: uniforms, overalls, helmets, staff IDs and the site bus. Both share one line: building with precision, delivering with confidence.
