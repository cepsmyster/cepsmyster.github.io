Oncology infusion center presentation

Client
Touch ID Decoration
Work
Presentation design
Sector
Healthcare interiors
Year
2026

A client presentation for the renovation of a hospital’s Level 6E oncology infusion center.

The deck takes the client from the floor plan of 26 single rooms into 3D concept views of the reception, patient rooms and the details a patient actually touches: bed controls, call buttons, door signs.

I kept the layout quiet, with one white frame and a small header on every slide, so the renders carry the presentation.
