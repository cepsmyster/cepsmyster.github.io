Ads and brochures for a real estate brokerage

Client
Daytona Properties
Work
Print, social media ads
Sector
Real estate
Year
2024–2026

A leasing brochure, ads and stories for Daytona Properties, a Dubai real estate brokerage.

Valoura Residences, a new building in Nad Al Sheba that Daytona manages, needed a brochure. Six pages take a tenant from the cover to the inquiry form: location and drive times, the building, unit types from studios to two-bedroom apartments, and the amenities, closing on a QR code to book a viewing.

Day to day, I made the ads and stories that bring buyers in: launches like Binghatti Skyhall and DAMAC Islands, and a series that asks investors whether their returns come from real data. Navy, a classic serif and generous space keep it all recognisably Daytona.
