Work

Sector

Year
2026

A website I designed and built for an architecture and engineering consultancy in Dubai.

The home page opens on a tower drawn as a blueprint on one side and the finished render on the other. Visitors drag the line between them, from concept to completion, while the headline draws itself in outline and fills in.

Behind it sit a filterable project archive with a page for every project, an expertise page, blogs, careers and contact forms, in a dark and a light theme. It’s plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages.
