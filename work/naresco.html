Social media and print for a contractor

Client
Naresco Contracting
Work
Social media, print
Sector
Construction
Year
2024–2026

A consistent visual language for Naresco Contracting, a contractor with a 40-year history.

I focused on building a consistent visual language across every touchpoint, from social media content to print. Whether it was technical graphics or marketing collateral, everything had to reflect Naresco’s long-standing reputation for quality.

The occasion posts for Eid, Labour Day and National Day pair Arabic calligraphy with cranes and skylines drawn in the brand blue, so the greetings still look like a builder’s.
