Client

Work
Social media, motion, video and photo coverage
Sector
Exhibition
Year

Countdown stories, video and photo coverage for The Top Group at IPS 2026, Dubai World Trade Centre.

Each countdown story keeps to three things: one big number, the dates and the booth. Every company in the group signs off underneath: TCI, TCI Interiors, TCI Landscape, X30 Technical, Touch ID Decorations and TID Contracting.

At the show I filmed and photographed the stand, the meetings in the lounge, the tower model and the live violinist, then cut it all into a vertical recap for social media. On the stand, the backdrop video cycled through project sheets from across the group.
