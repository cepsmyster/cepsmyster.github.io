Countdown stories and the booth backdrop video for The Top Group at IPS 2026, Dubai World Trade Centre.

The countdown stories keep to one big number, the dates and the booth, with every company in the group signing off underneath: TCI, TCI Interiors, TCI Landscape, X30 Technical, Touch ID Decorations and TID Contracting.

On the stand, the backdrop video cycled through project sheets from across the group, interleaved with vertical reels from their Instagram pages.
