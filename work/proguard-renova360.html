Print, social and ads for two property service brands

Client

Work
Print, social media, ads
Sector

Year

Company profiles, social content and paid ads for two property service brands, ProGuard and ReNova360 Degrees.

I led the design of their core brand assets, most notably the company profiles, balancing technical detail with a clean, professional layout. Beyond print, I managed their ongoing visual presence with social media content and marketing collateral that stayed true to the new identity.

ProGuard’s ads pair line-illustrated technicians with loud, single-colour backgrounds so each service reads at a glance in a feed, in English and Arabic.
