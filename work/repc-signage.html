Storefront signage for a polyclinic academy

Client
Royal European Polyclinic Academy
Work
Signage, print
Sector
Healthcare and beauty
Year
2026

Opening soon and grand opening signage for the new storefront of Royal European Polyclinic Academy.

Two directions for the hoarding. One light, with script lettering and the treatment device as the hero. One deep navy, built on close-up treatment photography.

I mocked both up on a photo of the actual storefront, so the client could see them in place before anything went to print.
