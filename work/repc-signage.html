Royal European Polyclinic Academy signage

Client
Royal European Polyclinic Academy
Work
Signage, print
Sector
Healthcare and beauty
Year
2026

Opening soon and grand opening signage for a polyclinic academy’s new storefront.

Two directions for the hoarding: a light one with script lettering and the treatment device as the hero, and a deep navy one built on close-up treatment photography.

Both were mocked up on a photo of the actual storefront before going to print.
