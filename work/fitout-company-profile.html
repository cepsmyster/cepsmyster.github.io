Company profile for an interior fit-out company

Client
Top Concept International
Work
Editorial, presentation design
Sector
Interior fit-out and joinery
Year
2026

A 79-page company profile for a Dubai fit-out and joinery company in the group, made at Top Concept International.

After the about, vision and services pages, the profile sorts the work by sector: government, residential towers, offices, food and beverage, retail and landscape. Each sector opens on a full-width photo with a bold yellow headline and a short list of notable projects.

The project spreads that follow pair one large photo with a grid of smaller ones and a yellow caption block, so close to eighty pages keep one rhythm.
